# Response to hostile technical review

We agree that the defensible paper is a physical-plausibility and uncertainty study, not a molecular-mechanism proof. The final release resolves every identified computational category-A issue and preserves experimental gaps as limitations.

1. **Neural construct.** All 276 selected objects are nervous-manifest entries and pass actual-content watertightness, winding, and positive-interior checks after vertex merging. The numerator is the logical union of their exact interiors; overlaps count once. Four body-clipped pitches and a proxy density define the mass. We now write “analysis-only neural mean dose” and prohibit cell/membrane interpretations.

2–3. **Rare events and correlated ratio.** Two direct 100-million-history runs supply 1,264 focused and 318 diffuse neural events, effective counts 753 and 200, and relative SE 3.6% and 7.1%. Event-level covariance delta intervals are corroborated by 2,000 Poisson event bootstraps. Prefix and independent 10-million-run tests pass; all independent-replicate |z| values are ≤1.44.

4. **Registration.** The perturbations are labeled deterministic brackets, never confidence intervals. High-statistics ratios span 0.991–1.066 of baseline focused and 1.000–1.159 diffuse. Animal-derived registration requires imaging.

5. **Outliers.** Of 100,000 original-surface samples, 0.257% exceed 10 µm and 0.031% exceed 25 µm, predominantly posterior terminal processes. Reciprocal ROI-to-reference error is <0.25 µm. Exact/0.25-voxel membership swaps produce only −0.315% focused and −1.509% diffuse net energy differences. We explicitly disallow named-neurite dose.

6. **Surface-area control.** The final ensemble contains 99 full-resolution, identical-triangle rigid-atlas controls per irradiation, improving empirical p resolution to 0.01. The analysis does not support strong preferential neural targeting; the manuscript says so.

7. **Muscle asymmetry—fixed.** We added seven muscle-surface shells, 0–5 µm muscle scoring, local edep-weighted spectra, two muscle chemistry runs, and equal-basis muscle dose. Nervous and muscle surfaces each receive about 14.3% of whole deposition within 5 µm. Muscle dose is comparable or slightly higher than neural dose.

8. **Step assignment.** `G4StepLimiterPhysics` is registered and the active 0.5 µm charged limit is logged and verified in ROOT. Charged deposition uses bounded midpoints; neutral discrete deposition uses post-interaction positions. The prior inert-limit runs are superseded.

9. **Outside body.** The high-stat runs revealed 20 focused and 19 diffuse boundary-adjacent authoritative coordinates outside the body. The prespecified scorer excludes them. Their energies are 1.90×10−6 and 2.03×10−5 of whole deposition, explicitly reported. No such row enters regional scoring.

10. **Navigation.** Warnings occur at 2.26×10−5 and 4.35×10−6 per history, mainly body/digestive and body/muscle boundaries. Saved-step/event energy is exactly conserved. We retain pair counts and avoid anatomical erosion solely to suppress nonfatal pushes.

11–12. **Source and medium.** Soft/hard spectra and worm-only environments are tracked sensitivity cases. Diffuse M9/glass materially affects the perineural endpoint and is retained. Exact spectra, liquid thickness, and dosimetry are correctly assigned to experimental measurement rather than false computational precision.

13–14. **Chemistry normalization.** Six new 10,000-event chemistry cases use local electron-edep-weighted spectra and actual all-particle regional deposited energy. We do not reinterpret condensed-history steps as DNA ionizations. The result is a separate homogeneous-water yield reference.

15–17. **Chemical limits.** Every output is labeled a homogeneous-water molecule equivalent. The OH/Trp and OH/Cys rates are primary free-solute measurements and are swept across target/scavenging brackets. H2O2 inhibition/reset evidence is included alongside Cys/PRDX/coincidence evidence. No encounter is equated to site modification.

18. **Gating/behavior.** No activation, open probability, calcium, or behavioral response metric exists. Exposure reuse is explicitly fluence-linear physics, not a response fit.

19. **Animal variation.** One-atlas morphology and registration are stated limitations requiring experimental images. Increased histories cannot supply population anatomy.

20. **Dosimetry.** Experimental 0.5–2 scaling is a separate interval on absolute Gy and molecule equivalents. It is never quadrature-combined with Monte Carlo uncertainty.
