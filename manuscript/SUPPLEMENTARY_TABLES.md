# Supplementary tables

The authoritative numeric tables are machine-readable and regenerated by `ros_worm_stage1/scripts/final/build_final_tables.py`.

- `validation/final/tables/final_nominal_regional_dose.csv`: final neural and muscle estimators, covariance/bootstraps, effective event counts, and 95% intervals.
- `validation/final/tables/final_uncertainty_budget.csv`: Monte Carlo, ROI, registration, physical-input, and dosimetry intervals kept separate.
- `validation/final/tables/final_cannon_condition_table.csv`: all focused and diffuse experimental conditions with neural/muscle dose, perineural energy, OH/H2O2 equivalents, and Level-1 target ranges.
- `validation/final/tables/neural_muscle_surface_edep_shells.csv`: seven distance shells for both anatomical surfaces.
- `validation/final/tables/longitudinal_edep_profiles.csv`: 20 µm spatial profiles.
- `validation/final/tables/transport_qc_and_navigation.csv`: energy conservation, containment exclusions, and warning rates.
- `validation/final/neural_roi/`: member, volume, surface-error, outlier, and membership-disagreement audits.
- `validation/final/chemistry/`: run index, full species time courses, exposure scaling, and target sweeps.

## Interpretation rules

“Neural dose” always means the documented analysis-only source-object union. “Perineural energy” means distance to the original surface and has no neural mass. “Molecule equivalent” means homogeneous-water yield from local energy. “Target interaction opportunity” is not LITE-1 activation.
